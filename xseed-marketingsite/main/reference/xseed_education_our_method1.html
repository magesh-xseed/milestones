OUR METHOD

Learning becomes powerful when children actively participate.

The XSEED Method turns learning into a cycle of experience, thinking, application and reflection — helping children understand deeply and act independently.

“Experience. Think. Apply. Grow.”
01 — FROM TEACHING TO LEARNING

Children don't just listen. They take part.

Instead of only explaining concepts, learning begins with meaningful experiences that make children observe, question, discuss and discover.

The child becomes an active learner, building intuitive understanding before formal vocabulary is defined.

ORIGINS & RESEARCH

Where the XSEED Method comes from.

The XSEED Method grew out of years of classroom research and iteration led by Ashish Rajpal, an alumnus of the Harvard Graduate School of Education, with a single mission: to make every child a genuine learner, not just an exam performer.

Now implemented in 3,500+ schools across India and globally, it remains one of the most rigorously tested mastery-based pedagogies in Indian education.

“Every child a genuine learner.”
02 — THE XSEED METHOD

A simple rhythm for deeper learning.

Aim → Action → Analysis → Application → Assessment

  1. 1
    STEP 01 — AIM

    Set the Purpose

    Know what children need to understand and be able to do. Every class opens with a clear goal, put into words the children choose themselves.

  2. 2
    STEP 02 — ACTION

    Experience First

    Children run an activity or a small experiment first; ideas and terms are named only afterward.

  3. 3
    STEP 03 — ANALYSIS

    Think & Discuss

    Children talk through what happened and why, guided by questions rather than given the answer.

  4. 4
    STEP 04 — APPLICATION

    Use Independently

    Practice problems ask children to use the idea somewhere new, on their own.

  5. 5
    STEP 05 — ASSESSMENT

    Guide Next Steps

    A quick, low-stakes check closes the loop so both child and teacher know what's next.

03 — EXPERIENCE FIRST

Understanding begins with doing.

Children learn through activities, examples, conversations, experiments and real situations.

They experience the idea before being asked to remember it.

“Do first. Understand deeply.”
04 — MAKE THINKING VISIBLE

The important part is not only the answer.

It is how children think. Teachers ask questions that encourage children to explain, compare, reason, connect ideas and learn from one another.

“Question. Explain. Reflect.”
05 — APPLY INDEPENDENTLY

Learning matters when children can use it themselves.

Children apply their understanding to new questions, situations and challenges. This is where knowledge begins to become capability.

“From knowing → to doing.”
06 — FEEDBACK & RETRY

Mistakes become part of the learning process.

Children receive clear feedback, reflect on their work and get another opportunity to improve.

“Try. Reflect. Improve. Try again.”
07 — THE TEACHER'S ROLE

Teachers don't do the learning for children.

They create the conditions that make meaningful learning possible.

  • Create purposeful experiences
  • Ask before telling
  • Observe children's thinking
  • Give useful feedback
  • Encourage reflection & gradually release responsibility
“Guide more. Tell less.”

TEACHER & ADULT FACILITATION

Children do the learning.
Adults create the conditions.

Ask Before Telling

Instead of delivering answers, teachers prompt students with targeted questions that ignite curiosity and self-directed reasoning.

Structured Scaffolding

Provide guided frameworks and physical manipulatives that allow students to stretch their cognitive abilities safely.

Diagnostic Feedback

Feedback focuses on the thinking process rather than simple grade scores, giving clear actionable steps on how to improve.

Space for Iteration

Normalize wrong answers as natural stepping stones. Students are always given room to adjust their work and try again.

Make Thinking Visible

Encourage students to articulate their reasoning out loud or draw visual models to clarify internal logic.

Gradual Release of Control

As student confidence grows, teachers step back, empowering students to take full ownership of their intellectual journey.

Student Discovery (THINK & QUESTION): "3 parts out of 4 total parts are red. That's 3 out of 4!"

01 / Aim

Aim

Where are we going?

Every learning journey begins with a goal worth reaching. Students know what they are solving before the activity starts, so the lesson has direction from the first minute.

Set the goal
02 / Action

Action

Try it with your hands and mind.

Children learn by doing. They move, test, adjust, and explore possibilities instead of waiting for the teacher to complete the thinking on their behalf.

Learn by doing
03 / Analysis

Analysis

What happened, and why?

Reflection converts activity into understanding. Students discuss observations, compare outcomes, and derive the underlying idea with clarity.

Make meaning
04 / Application

Application

Now use it somewhere new.

Real learning travels. Students carry the idea into a new problem and prove that knowledge has become a usable skill rather than a one-time classroom answer.

Use it beyond the lesson
05 / Assessment

Assessment

Measure progress. Strengthen what comes next.

Assessment shows what a child can now do. It closes the loop, surfaces understanding, and informs the next move in the learning journey.

Close the loop
